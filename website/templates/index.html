How relevance works

Baseline score
0–100
A normalized popularity score relative to the most-interacted item in the returned results. It is not a probability of purchase.
Known limitation
The dataset contains item IDs but no product names, descriptions, prices, or categories. The popularity baseline is not a personalized recommender.
